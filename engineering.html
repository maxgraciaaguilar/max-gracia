Wind vs. Nuclear

Honors project · May 2026

An interdisciplinary analysis comparing wind power with natural gas backup against small modular nuclear reactors for a median American city. Each scenario is evaluated on land footprint and dual-use potential, ecological impact, lifecycle carbon dioxide emissions, capacity factor, upfront and operating costs, fuel security, construction timeline, transmission infrastructure, and risk assessment.

Daft Punk Helmets

Halloween costume · October 2026

A full-scale replica of both Daft Punk helmets, built as wearable, fully working costumes. The project covers mechanical design, surface finishing, and embedded electronics: hand-finished 3D-printed shells, vacuum-formed tinted visors, and custom firmware for addressable LED displays. One helmet runs real-time audio spectrum analysis, and the other takes scrolling text from a phone over Bluetooth. The power system is battery-powered and runs within a set current limit.

SOS Beacon

Treobytes · July 2026

A low-cost Arduino beacon kit that middle school students build themselves in a hands-on STEM program and carry on an outdoor hike. The project went through a full engineering design process: an initial design report, measurable requirements, and preliminary, critical, and final design reviews. I designed the enclosure in CAD and produced it with 3D printing and UV printing. I also soldered the components and mass-produced 60 complete kits. Each beacon uses a solderless breadboard circuit with an RGB LED, a speaker, two potentiometers, and a push button, so students can assemble it without tools and then write their own custom features on top of the base code. The snap-fit enclosure clips to a backpack, runs on a single 9V battery, and costs about $14 per kit. A student assembly guide and bill of materials let each build fit in a single class session.
