Wind vs. Nuclear

Honors project · May 2026

An interdisciplinary analysis comparing wind power with natural gas backup against small modular nuclear reactors for a median American city. Each scenario is evaluated on land footprint and dual-use potential, ecological impact, lifecycle carbon dioxide emissions, capacity factor, upfront and operating costs, fuel security, construction timeline, transmission infrastructure, and risk assessment.

Daft Punk Helmets

Halloween costume - October 2026

A full-scale replica of both Daft Punk helmets, built as wearable, fully working costumes. The project covers mechanical design, surface finishing, and embedded electronics: hand-finished 3D-printed shells, vacuum-formed tinted visors, and custom firmware for addressable LED displays. One helmet runs real-time audio spectrum analysis, and the other takes scrolling text from a phone over Bluetooth. The power system is battery-powered and runs within a set current limit.
